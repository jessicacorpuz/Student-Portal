Frequently Asked
Questions

Grades & Academics

Q: When will my final grades be visible on the portal?

Grades are only visible to students and parents once the subject professor has finalized and encoded them, and the Academic Dean has approved the submission. If a specific subject column is blank, it means the grade is still undergoing faculty evaluation.

Q: I found an error in my encoded grades. What should I do?

Students cannot edit grade records. If you believe there is a clerical error on your report card, do not submit an IT ticket. Instead, contact your instructor directly so they can file an official Grade Correction Form with the Registrar's Office.

Accounts & Login Issues

Q: I forgot my password. How can I reset it?

Students & Parents: Click the "Forgot Password?" link on the login page. Enter your registered student ID or mobile number to receive a one-time reset link via SMS or personal email.

Faculty & Staff: Password resets for faculty must be processed through the campus IT Helpdesk for security compliance. Please visit the IT office or submit a ticket using your institutional email.

Accounts & School Fees

Q: How long does it take for my payment to reflect on my portal balance?

Online/Bank Payments: Payments made via Dragonpay, online bank transfers, or partner payment centers take 24 to 48 hours to validate and sync with your portal dashboard.

On-Campus Cashier: Payments made directly at the school cashier's window will reflect on your student account instantly.

Subject Enrollment & Dropping

Q: Can I change my schedule or drop a subject directly through the portal?

During Regular Registration: Yes, students can use the Subject Enrollment module to select their preferred blocks and sections, provided they meet the prerequisites.

After the Enrollment Period: The self-enrollment feature will lock. Any further adding, dropping, or changing of subjects must be processed manually through your academic adviser or the Registrar.

Q: Why am I getting a "Prerequisite Not Met" error during enrollment?

The system checks your academic history automatically. You cannot enroll in advanced IT or major subjects if you haven't passed or completed the required introductory classes from the previous semesters.

Additional Questions:

How do I enroll?

Log in to the Student Portal, go to your Student Dashboard, and use the Subject Enrollment module during the official registration period to pick your subjects and section.

Where is the registrar?

The Registrar's Office is at the ICCT Colleges Building, Sumulong Extension Street, Brgy. San Roque, Antipolo City, Rizal.

What are the tuition fees?

Tuition varies by program and year level. Check the Accounting section of your portal or visit the cashier for your specific fee assessment.

What documents are required?

Typical requirements include Form 137/138, a Certificate of Good Moral Character, and a valid ID. Confirm the full checklist with the Admissions Office for your enrollment type.
